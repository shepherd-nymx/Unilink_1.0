# UniLink store listing draft

**App name (30-character limit):** UniLink

**Short description (80-character limit):** Order groceries from local shops and connect with delivery messengers.

**Category suggestion:** Shopping

## Full description draft

Make grocery errands fit around your campus day with UniLink.

Choose your campus or delivery zone, browse groceries and essentials from local shops, and place an order for a messenger to deliver to your door.

With UniLink you can:
- Order groceries and everyday essentials.
- Follow order progress and chat with your messenger in the app.
- Review your delivery experience.
- Register as a messenger to accept deliveries in your service area.
- Access a shop-owner portal to manage participating store activity.

Delivery availability depends on participating shops, active messengers and your selected service area. Prices, payment options and delivery charges are shown in the app where applicable.

Visit https://www.unilinkbase.com to learn more.
