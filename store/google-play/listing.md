## Full description

UniLink connects shoppers, local shops and delivery messengers in one marketplace.

Browse registered shops and their products, find shops that have shared their location on the map, and place orders from your phone. Choose pickup or available delivery options and review the charges shown before confirming your order.

For shoppers:
- Discover local shops and browse their available products.
- View participating shops on the map.
- Place orders and follow their progress.
- Communicate about your order through in-app messaging.
- Manage your profile and saved delivery details.

For shops:
- Register your shop and manage its profile.
- Add products, update availability and manage incoming orders.
- Share your shop's location to help customers find it.

For messengers:
- Register a messenger profile.
- View available delivery requests and manage accepted deliveries.
- Communicate with customers about deliveries.

Shop availability, products, payment methods and delivery coverage depend on the participating businesses and location. Location and camera access are requested for features that need them.

Need help? Contact unilinkbase@gmail.com.
